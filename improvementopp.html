Task Overview: Impact vs. Effort Evaluation

In digital transformation and process improvement, organisations must balance business value against time, budget, and implementation difficulty. Your objective is to triage the 8 candidate initiatives below across two strategic dimensions:

• Quick Wins (High Impact, Low Effort): Deploy first. Delivers rapid results, minimal technical barrier, and early team buy-in.
• Strategic (High Impact, High Effort): Plan & budget. Major long-term gains requiring structured project planning.
• Fill-Ins (Low Impact, Low Effort): Execute when time permits. Small, low-risk procedural adjustments.
• Time Sinks (Low Impact, High Effort): De-prioritise or avoid. High cost or effort with very little practical return.

Task Overview: MoSCoW Scope & Governance Classification

To keep projects on schedule and avoid scope creep, categorize each proposed initiative into an agreed project release tier:

• Must Have: Non-negotiable requirements, legal compliance, safety checks, or essential baseline functionality.
• Should Have: Important operational improvements delivering clear business value, but with short-term workarounds available.
• Could Have: Useful additions that improve user convenience, but can be safely postponed if time runs short.
• Won't Have (Now): Agreed as out of scope for the current rollout to protect deadlines and budget.
