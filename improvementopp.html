Task Overview: Impact vs. Effort Evaluation

In digital transformation and process automation, organisations face finite budgets, delivery windows, and engineering capacity. Your objective is to triage the 8 candidate initiatives below across two strategic dimensions:

• Quick Wins (High Impact, Low Effort): Deploy first. Immediate ROI, low complexity, rapid stakeholder buy-in.
• Strategic (High Impact, High Effort): Plan & budget. Transformative gains requiring phased milestones.
• Fill-Ins (Low Impact, Low Effort): Execute when spare capacity permits. Minor incremental gains.
• Time Sinks (Low Impact, High Effort): De-prioritise or avoid. High technical complexity for negligible return.

Task Overview: MoSCoW Scope & Governance Classification

To prevent scope creep and manage operational constraints, categorize each proposed initiative into an agile MoSCoW governance release tier:

• Must Have: Non-negotiable statutory requirements, data governance, safety, or core functionality.
• Should Have: High-value operational improvements delivering strong ROI where workarounds exist.
• Could Have: Desirable enhancements that offer convenience but can be dropped if timelines compress.
• Won't Have (Now): Explicitly agreed as out of scope to protect deadlines and eliminate liability.
