Operational Excellence: Dual-Sector Multi-Task Triage Lab

The Situation: Broken Bistro

You are running a popular city-centre restaurant during Friday peak service. Operations are failing across floor workflows and digital backends. Complete all 5 triage tasks below to turn the restaurant around.

Task 1: Floor Order Bottlenecks

Waiters waste valuable time double-handling paper pads and walking to fixed terminals.

Task 2: Reservation Synchronization

Booking logs on third-party apps and the front desk clash, causing double-bookings.

Task 3: Inventory Re-ordering Friction

Chefs spend hours manually counting stock and keying paper delivery invoices into spreadsheets.

Task 4: Payment Authorisation Delays

Staff waste time chasing duty managers to swipe keycards for minor bill splits and discounts.

Task 5: Kitchen Allergen Safety Tracking

Dietary requirements noted during reservation are frequently missed on kitchen handwritten tickets.

The Situation: The Tangled Tech Desk

You are the Lead DevOps & IT Operations Manager for a growing SaaS enterprise. Diagnose and solve all 5 release and infrastructure bottlenecks below.

Task 1: Manual Deployment Queue

Releases require manual multi-day code reviews and sluggish QA sign-off queues.

Task 2: Cloud Traffic Scaling Failures

Static cloud servers crash and timeout during sudden traffic surges.

Task 3: Environment Drift & Inconsistencies

"It works on my machine" bugs occur because staging and production setups differ.

Task 4: Incident Response Blindspots

Production errors are only discovered when angry clients submit support tickets hours later.

Task 5: Security & Compliance Audits

Security vulnerability checks happen only right before major annual product launches.
