Apprentice Workshop: Linear vs Swimlane Process Modelling

Step 1: Select Your Mapping Methodology

Before mapping a task from your workplace, determine whether your process is straightforward and sequential, or involves multiple departments and hand-offs.

Linear Process Map

Sequential End-to-End Flow

A chronological sequence of steps showing how a task flows from a specific trigger to a final outcome. Best for single-person workflows or straightforward standard operating procedures (SOPs).

  • Focuses on chronological actions (Start $\rightarrow$ Step $\rightarrow$ End).
  • Identifies bottlenecks, delays, and approval gates.
  • Ideal for simple administrative tasks or data entry workflows.
Swimlane Process Map

Cross-Functional Swimlane Flow

Maps processes across different roles, departments, or systems (lanes). Crucial for highlighting hand-offs, communication gaps, and accountability across a wider business operation.

  • Separates tasks into distinct horizontal or vertical lanes (e.g., Customer $\rightarrow$ Sales $\rightarrow$ Finance).
  • Exposes delays caused by inter-departmental hand-offs.
  • Essential for complex procurement, onboarding, or IT support workflows.
