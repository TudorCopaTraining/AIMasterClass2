The Situation: Struggling Talent Recruitment Operations

You are the Operations Director at a high-volume staffing agency. Candidate drop-off is at an all-time high of 42%, consultants spend 60% of their workday copy-pasting CV data between spreadsheets, and client hiring managers complain of sluggish placement cycle times. Complete all 5 triage tasks below to streamline talent operations.

Task 1: Inbound CV Intake & Parsing Bottlenecks

Consultants spend 15 hours weekly manually downloading attached PDF/Word resumes from shared inboxes and re-keying candidate profiles into an internal tracker.

Task 2: Candidate Interview Scheduling Clashes

Recruiters play email ping-pong across 4 to 6 back-and-forth messages with candidates and client hiring managers to book 30-minute screening calls, resulting in frequent double-bookings.

Task 3: Right-to-Work (RTW) & Compliance Document Chasing

Placement start dates are consistently delayed because recruiters forget to request passport copies, visas, and proof of address until after the job offer is accepted.

Task 4: Consultant Fee Split & Commission Sign-Off Friction

Commission reconciliations take 5 working days at month-end because split placements require manual manager spreadsheet sign-offs and physical printed signature slips.

Task 5: Rejected Candidate "Ghosting" & Brand Erosion

Over 80% of unsuccessful applicants receive zero feedback after screening because consultants have no time to compose individual regret emails, causing poor Glassdoor reviews.

The Situation: Stalled B2B Sales Operations

You are the Commercial Operations Manager for an enterprise technology vendor. High-value inbound leads go cold before sales reps make initial contact, quote generation takes 4 business days, and CRM data integrity is severely compromised. Diagnose and solve all 5 sales pipeline bottlenecks below.

Task 1: Inbound Lead Triage & Speed-to-Lead Latency

Website enterprise demo requests sit in an unmonitored mailbox for up to 48 hours before an administrator manually assigns them to sales reps via forwarded email.

Task 2: Swivel-Chair CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) Friction

Reps spend 3 hours per prospect manually calculating discounts in custom desktop spreadsheets and re-typing customer line items into Microsoft Word contract templates.

Task 3: Discount Authorisation Bottlenecks

Quotes with minor 5% discount requests stall in executive inboxes for days, delaying deal closing and driving buyers to competitors.

Task 4: Post-Meeting CRM Hygiene & Activity Logging

Account executives fail to log meeting notes and next steps into the CRM because the 15-field desktop CRM form is clunky and slow on mobile.

Task 5: Closed-Won Handover to Customer Success

When enterprise contracts close, customer implementation teams receive fragmented details via email, resulting in duplicated onboarding kickoff calls and client frustration.
