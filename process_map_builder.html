Task Overview: As-Is Process Mapping & Stakeholder Flow

Before introducing artificial intelligence or workflow automation, practitioners must accurately capture the baseline reality of how work is completed today. An 'As-Is' Swimlane Process Map provides transparency by illustrating chronological sequence, clear role accountability, and departmental handoffs.

1. Swimlane Ownership Horizontal lanes represent specific stakeholders or teams. Placing a step in a lane explicitly defines who holds operational responsibility for that action.
2. Standard Geometric Notation Ovals represent starting triggers and terminal ends. Rectangles represent action tasks. Diamonds indicate decision gateways.
